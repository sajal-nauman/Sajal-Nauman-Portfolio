Featured Projects

Power Electronics

30W USB Fast Charger (SMPS)

AC Input→ Bridge Rectifier & Filter→ PWM Switching, TL494→ Isolation Transformer→ Rectify & Regulate, LM2596→ 5V / 6A Output

A switch-mode supply that steps 220V AC mains down to a regulated 5V rail at up to 6A. The TL494 controller drives a MOSFET at high frequency, keeping the transformer small while still delivering a full 30W efficiently — the same principle behind compact modern fast chargers.

Power Electronics

5V DC Regulated Power Supply

AC Input→ Step-Down Transformer→ Bridge Rectifier→ Smoothing Capacitor→ 7805 Regulator→ 5V Output + LED

A classic linear supply: the transformer steps mains voltage down, a bridge rectifier converts it to pulsating DC, a 2200µF capacitor smooths the ripple, and a 7805 regulator locks the output to a clean, stable 5V — confirmed by an onboard LED indicator.

Digital Logic

Digital Dice Roller

Push Button→ 555 Astable Clock→ 4017 Decade Counter→ Diode Pattern Encoder→ 4511 BCD-7Seg Driver→ 7-Segment Display

Holding the button feeds a fast clock from a 555 timer into a 4017 counter, cycling through its ten outputs many times a second. A diode network maps those states onto valid dice faces, and a 4511 decoder latches the result onto the display the instant the button is released — then built and tested on breadboard, shown here.

Digital Logic

Elevator Floor Control System

Floor Request, Switches→ 74LS174 Register→ 74LS85 Comparator→ Floor Reached→ AND Gate→ Arrival Bell

A requested floor set on input switches is latched into a 74LS174 register and shown on one display, standing in for the call button press. Separately, AND/OR/NOT gate logic clocks a 74LS190 counter that steadily cycles through floor values on a second display, modeling the car's current position. A 74LS85 magnitude comparator continuously checks the requested floor against the current one, and the instant they match, an AND gate trips the arrival bell — a simplified digital model of how a controller decides exactly when to stop at the floor you asked for.
